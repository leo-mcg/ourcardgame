On the table

Three games, ready to deal

Built to be a generic deck and table, with some favourite games set up and ready to play.

♠

Estimation

Each round, estimate how many of the 13 tricks you’ll win, then try to win exactly that many.

4 players at a time, with up to 6 rotating in after each round.

★

Wiz

A lot like Estimation, but starting with 1 card in the first round, plus Wizard and Jester cards. Also known as “Wizard”.

3 to 6 players can play at the same time.

♥

Pinochle

The classic American card game, right at your virtual table.

4 players.

Guests can watch, too. Invite friends to sit on the rail. If the admin turns on “show cards to non-players,” watchers can see every hand and study how each player plays to win.

How it works

From “wanna play?” to first deal in four steps

  1. 1

    Create a game

    Click Start and set up your table. You’re the admin, so you enter the players’ names and choose the settings. You can make other people admins too.

  2. 2

    Share the private link

    You get a private web address for your game. Send it however you usually share things, like email, WhatsApp or a shared doc. It’s cryptic, so don’t read it out over the phone!

  3. 3

    Claim your seat

    Everyone opens the same link and claims their spot by name. No passwords, no accounts, no sign-ups.

  4. 4

    Hop on a call & deal

    Get everyone on a video or audio call using your favourite app. If you don’t have one, the system can make a free Jitsi room for your game.

No catch

Free, private, and right in your browser

Our Card Game started as a personal project for friends to play their favourite games. Since June 2020, it’s been free for everyone.

  • Any current browser

    Use it on a Windows or Linux laptop or desktop. It also works on most iPhones, iPads, Android phones and tablets, MacBooks and Chromebooks. Just give it a quick test first.

  • Nothing to sign up for

    There are no logins and no email collected. The system knows nothing about you, and your game stays in the cloud until you delete it.

  • Keep the bragging rights

    Copy the Score Sheet and paste it into an email or Excel to share. Or gloat in your victory.

Questions & answers

Before you shuffle up

Quick answers here. The full FAQ covers admins, testing on your own, and more.

Read the full FAQ →
How much does it cost?

Nothing. It’s a personal project, and since June 2020 it has been free for anyone to use.

How many players can there be?

Most games are for four people. Estimation plays 4 at a time with up to 6 rotating, Wiz plays 3 to 6, and Pinochle is for 4. Guests can also watch.

How do my friends join?

Share your game’s private web address. They open it and claim their spot by name. If someone claims the wrong name, they can switch.

Can I test it by myself?

Yes. Open a second “incognito” or “private” tab, or another browser, and connect to your game with the same address.
